and others — the recompense for «what it earned, and they will not be wronged» (Q. 3:161). Then it [the cloak] turned up in the possession of a man. «Is one who follows the good pleasure of Allah» — who obeyed and did not defraud — «like one who has brought upon himself» — returned with — «wrath from Allah» — for his disobedience — «and whose refuge is Hell? And evil is the destination» (Q. 3:162). «They are degrees» — possessors of degrees — «with Allah» — differing in their stations. So for the one who follows His good pleasure there is reward, and for the one who brings upon himself His wrath there is punishment. On the Day of Resurrection banners will be raised: the banner of truthfulness for Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, with the veracious (ṣiddīqūn) all beneath it; and the banner of justice for ʿUmar, so that every just commander will be beneath ʿUmar’s banner; and those who spend, their banner is in the hand of ʿUthmān; and the ulama, their banner is in the hand of ʿAlī, may Allah ennoble his face; and the banner of the reciters is in the hand of Ubayy b. Kaʿb; and the banner of the call to prayer (adhān) is in the hand of Bilāl; and so likewise every people of a quality have a banner that is in the hand of their chief among the Companions. These are the degrees that Allah mentioned in this verse. «And Allah is seeing of what they do» (Q. 3:163). «Allah has indeed conferred a favour upon the believers in that He sent among them a messenger from among themselves» — Allah honoured the believers when He sent among them an Arab messenger like themselves, so that they might understand from him and be ennobled through him, and not an angel and not a non-Arab — «who recites to them His verses» — the Qurʾan — «and purifies them» — cleanses them of sins — «and teaches them the Book and wisdom» — the sunna — «though they were before» — before his being sent — «in manifest error» (Q. 3:164). Before the sending of the Prophet ﷺ the Arabs were in manifest error. They had no distinction before the Messenger ﷺ and none before the Qurʾan. Their excellence, then, is by following the Qurʾan and the Messenger, and by nothing else. «Or when a misfortune struck you» — at Uḥud, with seventy of you killed — «you had already inflicted twice the like of it» — at Badr, with seventy killed and seventy taken captive — «you said» — in astonishment — «How» — from where has this come to us — «is this?» — this forsaking: how does Allah, Blessed and Exalted, forsake us when we are Muslims and the Messenger of Allah is among us? — «Say: it is from yourselves» — the forsaking came only from you; you are the ones who left the post the Prophet ﷺ had fixed for you — «Indeed Allah is powerful over everything» (Q. 3:165). Allah is powerful over everything, and among that is the giving of help and the withholding of it; and He has requited you for your contravention.

إن كان الجلوس يمنع من الموت فإياكم والموت لا تموتوا أبدا وأنتم لابد ميتون (وَلا تَحْسِبَنَّ ألذِينَ فُتِلُوا) استشهدوا (مِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَمْوَاتاً بَلَ احْيَآءً عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ) أرواحهم في حواصل طير خضر تسرح في الجنة حيث شاءت كما ورد في الحديث[78] (يُرْزَقُونَ) يأكلون من ثمار الجنة (بَرِحِيَ بِمَا ءَاتِيْهُمْ أللَّهُ صِ بَصْلِهِ وَ) هم {(يَسْتَبْشِرُونَ بِالذِيرَ لَمْ يَلْحَقُواْ بِهِم مِنْ خَلْعِهمة← من إخوانهم (أَلَّ خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ) هم طلبوا من اللّٰه العود إلى الدنيا حتى يبشروا المسلمين أن من استشهد يجد كفاء من الكرامات، قال اللّٰه تبارك وتعالى أما الرجوع إلى الدنيا بعد الموت فلا سبيل إليه ولكن أنا أبلغ عنهم فقالوا بلغوا إخواننا المسلمين أننا قد لقينا ربنا فرضي عنا ورضينا عنه. اللهم صل على سيدنا محمد وسلم.

ENGLISH:
If sitting back keeps death away, then beware of death: do not die at all, ever — and yet you must inevitably die. «And do not think those who were killed» — who were martyred — «in the way of Allah to be dead; rather they are alive with their Lord» — their souls are in the crops of green birds that range through the Garden wherever they wish, as comes in the hadith — «provided for» (Q. 3:169) — they eat of the fruits of the Garden — «rejoicing in what Allah has given them of His bounty, and» — they — «they receive good tidings concerning those who have not joined them, left behind them» — of their brothers — «that no fear shall be upon them, nor shall they grieve» (Q. 3:170). They asked Allah for a return to this world, so that they might give the Muslims the good news that whoever is martyred finds a full recompense of karāmāt (saintly miracles). Allah, Blessed and Exalted, said: as for the return to this world after death, there is no way to it; but I shall convey word on their behalf. So they said: convey to our brothers the Muslims that we have met our Lord, and He is pleased with us and we are pleased with Him. O Allah, bless our master Muhammad and grant him peace.
