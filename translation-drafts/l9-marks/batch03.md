## ¶29  (markers to place: 23, 24)

ويتجرد ويغتسل ويلبس رداء وإزارا ونعلين فقط. الملك إذا دعا ضيفا وجاءه يأتي إلى بيته الخاص ويصافحه. فتأتي إلى بيت الله الحرام، والحجر الأسود هو يمين اللّٰه تبارك وتعالى، والخروج إلى الصفا والمروة وإلى منى وإلى المزدلفة وإلى عرفات، عادة الملوك إذا استدعوا كبيرا وأضافوه يخرجون معه للتنزه إلى محلاتهم وإلى بساتينهم وإلى ممالكهم هذا هو الخروج إلى منَّى، إلى عرفات والعود عند تمام الحج هو يوم المأدبة، هو يوم النحر. اللّه يقول "تَجْبِي إلَيْه ثمَرَات كُلِّ شَيء" حتى البشر، لهذا قال (وَلِلهِ عَلَى الناسِ حَجُّ الْبَيْتِ) أوجب اللّٰه على الناس حج هذا البيت (من إسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا ) طريقا فسره الرسول صلى اللّٰه عليه وسلم بالزاد والراحلة[23]، رواه الحاكم [24].

ENGLISH:
, stripping himself bare, washing, and putting on only an upper wrap, a waist-wrapper and a pair of sandals. When a king invites a guest and the guest comes to him, he comes to his private house and clasps his hand. So you come to the Sacred House of Allah, and the Black Stone is the right hand of Allah, Blessed and Exalted. And the going out to Ṣafā and Marwa, and to Minā, and to Muzdalifa, and to ʿArafāt: it is the custom of kings, when they summon a great man and host him, that they go out with him for recreation to their quarters, and to their orchards, and to their domains. This is the going out to Minā, to ʿArafāt. And the return, at the completion of the hajj, is the day of the banquet; it is the Day of Sacrifice. Allah says «there is brought to it the fruits of all things» (Q. 28:57) — even human beings. For this reason He said «And to Allah is owed, from the people, the pilgrimage to the House» — Allah has made obligatory upon the people the pilgrimage to this House — «for whoever is able to find a way to it» (Q. 3:97) — a way — which the Messenger ﷺ explained as provisions and a mount. Al-Ḥākim related it.

ENGLISH:
 — a hadith. «Say: O People of the Book, why do you disbelieve in the verses of Allah» — the Qurʾan — «and Allah is witness over what you do» (Q. 3:98) — and so He will requite you for it. «Say: O People of the Book» — the Jews and the Christians — «why do you turn away» — you divert — «from the way of Allah» — His religion — «him who has believed» — by your giving the lie to the Prophet ﷺ and concealing his description — «you seek it» — you seek the way — «crooked» — bent, that is, inclining away from the truth — «while you are witnesses» — knowing that the religion that is approved is Islam — «and Allah is not heedless of what you do» (Q. 3:99) — of unbelief and of giving the lie; He only defers you to your appointed time so that He may requite you. «O you who believe, if you obey a party of those who have been given the Book, they will turn you back, after your faith, into unbelievers» (Q. 3:100). The occasion of the revelation of this verse is that the Aws and the Khazraj were two tribes at enmity with one another for the span of a hundred and twenty years; they were continually at war. When Islam came and they met the Messenger ﷺ, the first to meet him were six of them, and they gave him their pledge. In the following year they confirmed the pledge of the six by sending twelve of them, and they gave him their pledge. And in the third year they sent seventy-three men and ten women

 and they confirmed the pledge at al-ʿAqaba. After this the Messenger ﷺ emigrated to Medina, and they all entered Islam; there was no one in Medina, of the Aws and the Khazraj, who did not enter Islam, and so the Messenger ﷺ named them Anṣār. Every city was conquered by the sword except the city of the Messenger, which was conquered by the Qurʾan alone; its people came to be called al-Anṣār, and you would not know an Awsī from a Khazrajī. There was a Jew living near them who had known their Jāhiliyya, their enmity and their warfare, and he hated the Muslims and envied them and attacked them a great deal. When he passed by a company of the Anṣār sitting together, brothers who loved one another, that angered him. So he went to a youth of his and said to him: I passed by a company of the Khazraj and a company of the Aws who are brothers loving one another. I would have you stand before them and say: Remember the Day of Buʿāth — that is a day of war that took place between them — on that day so-and-so and so-and-so of the Aws were killed, and so-and-so and so-and-so of the Khazraj were killed. And he recited the poems they used to recite to one another when they fought, until two men of them quarrelled, an Awsī and a Khazrajī, and they said: If that day were to return, we would do to you as we did in the past. And they said: We return to it now. So they called one another to arms. The Khazraj cried: To the Khazraj! and the Aws cried: To the Aws! They took up arms and meant to fight one another. The Messenger ﷺ went out to them with the Muhājirūn and said to them: ⟨With the cry of the Jāhiliyya, and I am here among you? Allah! Allah!⟩ So they threw down their swords and every one of them embraced his brother — and this was from al-shayṭān (Satan) — and they returned better than they had been before. And this verse was sent down concerning them; it takes in brothers in Allah until the end of time. «O you who believe, if you obey a party of those who have been given the Book» — the Jews — «they will turn you back, after your faith, into unbelievers.
