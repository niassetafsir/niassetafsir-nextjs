When the Prophet ﷺ complained of an illness he would recite the protective suras — al-Ikhlāṣ and the two suras of refuge — then breathe into his hand and wipe his head and whatever he could reach of his body, so much so that when he fell ill with the illness of his death ʿĀʾisha took to reciting the protective suras and breathing into the hands of the Messenger ﷺ, because the hands of the Messenger ﷺ are more blessed than her own hands, and to wiping, with the hands of the Messenger ﷺ, over the body of the Messenger ﷺ. And he said: ⟨Whoever among you is able to benefit his brother, let him do so.⟩ So then the forbidden incantation, the one that runs against reliance, is the incantation by foreign names, in which there may be the names of an idol or the names of their unbelieving kings, and that is shirk; this stands apart from faith. But as for [incantation] by the Qurʾan, by the [divine] names, and by Arabic supplications (adʿiya) whose meaning is understood, that does not run against reliance. «And it is not for a prophet to defraud» — the occasion of its revelation: a red velvet cloak went missing on the day of Badr, and some of the hypocrites said that perhaps it was the Prophet ﷺ who had taken it.
