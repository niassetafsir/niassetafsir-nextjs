ENGLISH:
«upon what they did» — rather, they desisted from it — «while they know» (Q. 3:135) — that what they had committed was an act of disobedience. This is the fourth category. «Those — their recompense is forgiveness from their Lord, and gardens beneath which rivers run, abiding therein» — that is, destined for everlasting abiding (khulūd) therein — «and excellent is the reward of those who work» (Q. 3:136) — in obedience. This is the reward. Whoever commits a sin and recites this verse, «And those who, when they commit an indecency or wrong themselves, remember Allah and ask forgiveness for their sins — and who forgives sins but Allah?» (Q. 3:135), and asks forgiveness of Allah, Blessed and Exalted, Allah forgives him. ⟨My servant, I shall not cease to forgive you so long as you keep asking My forgiveness. Were you to come to Me with sins enough to fill the earth and then ask My forgiveness, I would forgive you and not care.⟩ ⟨Had you not sinned, Allah would have taken you away and brought a people who sin and ask forgiveness, and He would forgive them.⟩ ⟨Had you not sinned, I would have feared for you something greater than it: self-admiration, self-admiration.⟩ Allah, Blessed and Exalted, does not love disobedience and does not love the disobedient, but He loves those who turn to Him in repentance; and those who turn to Him in repentance are none but the disobedient who repent (tāʾib). «There have passed away» — gone by — «before you ways» — courses taken with the unbelievers, in granting them respite and then seizing them — «So travel» — O believers — «in the land and see how the end of those who denied was» (Q. 3:137) — the messengers.
